About

Molood is an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Deputy General Director at MegaKLG, with over 13 years of professional experience spanning engineering and education — four years as a Circuit Designer and nine years in educational management and STEAM education at MegaKLG.

Her work sits at the intersection of engineering, education, teacher development, and practical learning, with a consistent focus on turning knowledge into meaningful, hands-on experiences for young learners.
